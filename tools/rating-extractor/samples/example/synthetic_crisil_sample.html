Rating Rationale. CRISIL Ratings has reaffirmed its 'CRISIL AAA/Stable' rating on the long term bank facilities of Bajaj Finance Limited. CRISIL Ratings has also reaffirmed the 'CRISIL A1+' rating on the short term bank facilities. The rated amount for the bank facilities is Rs.10,000 crore. Separately, the rating on the non convertible debenture programme of Rs.5,000 crore has been reaffirmed at 'CRISIL AAA/Stable' on 24-Mar-2026.

For reference, Bajaj Housing Finance Limited, a group company, carries a similar 'CRISIL AAA/Stable' rating, reflecting the strength of the parent.
